We’re Hiring: Senior Product Engineer

If you love designing and implementing easy-to-use, elegant tools used by hundreds of thousands of passionate people, I have an incredible opportunity for you! 

We’re looking for a senior product engineer. That is a full-stack developer with strong product sense.

Job details:
Salary: $70,000-120,000 + performance bonuses (Depending on experience & skills)
Position: Full-Time (remote)
Timezone: Flexible, with some overlap with Eastern Time
Mission: Create the most intuitive video editing tool UIs in the world

Our site: promptedit.com

How we work

Tech stack: Laravel, Vue, Inertia, Tailwind, Postgres, Typesense, AWS.

We’re a small, highly effective team. Light on process. We don’t have daily standups. We don’t do sprint planning or retro meetings. No planning poker or other ceremonies you might have seen at more traditional firms.

We have team meetings twice a week. Mondays and Thursdays around 11am Eastern Time. The rest of the time is flexible.

95% of your time will be spent building.

What we need from you

As a product engineer, you should be able to take vague and loosely defined features, ask useful questions, implement it, test it, and investigate problems after release. 

We’re looking for a self-motivated developer with high attention to detail. I’d like to feel safe merging your code without reviewing it. Some of your code will calculate prices users pay for AI generations. This requires a high degree of accuracy, otherwise we could lose money. 

We want you to help make our codebase easier to maintain and improve how we work. You'll have a lot of autonomy and real input into how features are built.

You need to be proficient in our tech stack already. At least 5 years with Laravel and Vue.

Nice to have

Bonus points for experience with video editing tools like Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve. Our existing DaVinci Resolve Extension is built on Electron. We might ask you to work on these.

We’re looking to add ClickHouse to our stack for product analysis, so experience with ClickHouse or other analytical databases would be advantageous.

DevOps or infra engineering. E.g. if you’ve set up Laravel to run on EKS or ECS. We’re looking to use ffmpeg in queue workers, which doesn’t work on Laravel Vapor or Laravel Cloud.

Maybe you have some skills you think we could gain from that we didn’t mention here. Please let us know.
